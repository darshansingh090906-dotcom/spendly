Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense details you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to provide and improve the service: to let you sign in, record your expenses, and show you summaries of your spending. We do not use your expense data for advertising.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share data if required by law, or with service providers who help us run Spendly and are bound to keep it confidential.

4. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorized access. We keep your data for as long as your account is active, and you may request deletion of your account and expenses at any time.

5. Your Rights

You can view, edit or delete the expenses you have entered, and you can ask us for a copy or removal of your personal information.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Spendly after changes take effect means you accept the revised policy.
